Questions parents ask

How do I get the Navayan app?

Scan the QR code on this page with your phone camera or search for ‘Navayan’ on Google Play. The app is for Android phones and will be available on Google Play soon.

What does the subscription include?

Three things in one subscription: the digital book in the app, 360 narrated lessons arranged as 6 levels × 20 units × 3 lessons, and the printed book delivered to your home.

How does the printed book reach my home?

After subscribing in the app, enter your home address and PIN code. The printed book is sent to that address. The address is used only to deliver the book.

I already have the book. What now?

The book has a 12-character activation code under the scratch panel (for example XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Register in the app with your mobile number and enter the code; the course opens right away. Each code works only once.

Do lessons work offline?

Yes, in the app. Download a unit first; its lessons then play without the internet and progress is saved on the phone. When you are back online, progress syncs automatically.

Does one subscription work for two children?

One mobile number has one account with a single progress record. Two children can learn on the same account, but their progress is not shown separately.

Where is children's data stored?

The account belongs to the parent; only the child's nickname and learning progress are stored. A detailed privacy notice will be published on this website.

What do schools get?

A plan for schools and tuition classes comes in a later phase. For now, parents can use the app and the book at home; schools can let us know through the contact section.
